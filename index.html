Your learning route

One gentle road from first lesson to licence

Everyone moves at their own speed, so there is no fixed timetable. Here is the general shape of the journey.

  1. 1

    First lesson

    We meet, chat about your goals and any worries, and take it from there in a quiet area.

  2. 2

    Gaining confidence

    Steering, junctions and traffic come together as practice makes them feel natural.

  3. 3

    Test ready

    Mock tests and focused practice on the things that matter most on the day.

  4. 4

    Licence!

    Independence, on your own terms. Then we are here for freeway or night driving if you want it.

FAQ

Good questions, honest answers

How many lessons will I need?

It varies a lot, depending on your experience and confidence. We will get a feel for it together in your first lesson.

Do you offer automatic and manual?

Yes, both. Let us know your preference when you get in touch.

Where do you pick me up?

From home, work or school within the local service area. If you are not sure whether we cover you, just ask.

I'm really nervous. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Nerves are normal, and the car is dual-control. We go at whatever pace feels comfortable.

What if I need to reschedule?

Please give as much notice as you can, then call or message and we will find another time.
